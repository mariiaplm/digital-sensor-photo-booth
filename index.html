How it works, and tips for better shots ⌄

What's actually happening

A hand-tracking model finds 21 points on each hand, 30 times a second. The app watches how those points move: fingertips above their knuckles means an open palm, and the gap between two index fingers becomes the corners of your frame.

Taking a shot

  • Hold the gesture steady — a three second countdown appears on screen.
  • The hand outlines disappear once the countdown starts, so they never end up in the photo.
  • A white flash and shutter sound means it's captured.
  • Then Save to download it, or Retake to try again.

Framing

  • A white rectangle is always on screen — that's exactly what gets saved.
  • Point both index fingers to drag out a different size. Move them apart for wider, closer for tighter.
  • Close one hand to throw that frame away and go back to the default.

If it isn't picking you up

  • More light helps most — hand tracking struggles in dim rooms.
  • Keep hands inside the camera view and roughly facing the lens.
  • Plain backgrounds beat busy ones.
  • Arm's length from the camera works better than very close.
